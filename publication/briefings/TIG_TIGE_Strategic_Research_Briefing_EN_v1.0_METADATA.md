## Purpose

This metadata record registers the English version 1.0 of the TIG / TIG-E Strategic Research Briefing.

The briefing is the primary orientation document for first-time scientific and institutional reviewers of the Integrity Nexus research ecosystem. It provides:

- a concise distinction between TIG and TIG-E;
- an overview of the physical research program and its current status;
- the relationship between the research program, the human-AI research infrastructure, and the connected repositories;
- the applicable claim, validation, and governance boundaries;
- the principal open research programs;
- and a structured entry point for qualified external assessment.

It is a communication, navigation, orientation, and review artifact.

It does not introduce a new physical derivation, change the scientific status of an existing result, close an Open Question, establish external validation, or modify a canonical theory object.
