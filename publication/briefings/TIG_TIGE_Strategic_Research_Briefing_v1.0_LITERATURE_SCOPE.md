- Canonical quantum-gravity work represents a major alternative route; TIG has not derived or replaced it.
- Thermodynamic/emergent-gravity approaches are related only at the level explicitly evidenced; no identity with TIG is claimed.
- Nonsingular black-hole models demonstrate a comparison class, not uniqueness, priority, or empirical confirmation of the TIG realization.
