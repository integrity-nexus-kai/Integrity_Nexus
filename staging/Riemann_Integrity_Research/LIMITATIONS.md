# Limitations and Non-Claims

This research initialization does not currently establish:

- a proof or disproof of the Riemann Hypothesis;
- a new theorem about zeros of the zeta function;
- completeness of its literature corpus;
- correctness of every factual statement contained in an intake source;
- a Hilbert–Pólya operator;
- a physical realization of the Riemann zeros;
- a valid bridge from TIG, Nexus, X5/X6, or other internal frameworks to classical analytic number theory;
- that any finite numerical verification extends to infinitely many zeros;
- that an audit verdict substitutes for a mathematical proof.

These non-claims remain active until individually superseded by explicit, evidence-supported records.
