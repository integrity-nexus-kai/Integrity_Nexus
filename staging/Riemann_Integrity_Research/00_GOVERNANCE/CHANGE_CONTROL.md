# Change Control

## 1. Historical immutability

Published or registered historical versions are not rewritten to make later understanding appear earlier.

## 2. Correction modes

Use one of:

- new version;
- explicit erratum/correction record;
- superseding artifact with backward link;
- refutation record.

## 3. Status promotion

Promotion requires an explicit record naming:

- object;
- previous state;
- target state;
- evidence;
- reviewer/audit basis where applicable;
- date;
- scope.

## 4. No deletion of failed mathematics by default

Failed proof attempts, counterexamples, and dead ends are research assets. Preserve them with explicit failure/refutation status instead of deleting them, unless deletion is required for security, privacy, copyright, or duplicate-file hygiene.
