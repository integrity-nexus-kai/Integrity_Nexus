# Provenance Policy

## Rule

Every load-bearing research object must be traceable backward to its inputs and forward to its uses.

Minimum chain:

```text
Source / Premise
→ Exact location
→ Research object
→ Transformation or inference
→ Result
→ Review / audit state
```

## Prohibited provenance failures

- invented or reconstructed citations presented as inspected;
- memory treated as source evidence;
- citation to a secondary source when a primary result is being claimed without disclosure;
- silent replacement of a source version;
- silent correction of a historical artifact;
- unattributed transfer from another repository or chat;
- copied formula with unknown assumptions;
- computational result without code/input/precision provenance.

## Chat-derived material

Chat material may be retained as a Working Base, design record, hypothesis, or research note. It is not mathematical evidence by origin alone. Chat-derived artifacts must identify date, origin, and preservation mode.
