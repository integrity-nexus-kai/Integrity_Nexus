# 0. Purpose of this baseline

This document freezes the current state of the Epistemic Provenance Audit so that the audit no longer depends on live conversational context.

The audit does not attempt to prove the scientific truth of every theory, mathematical claim, physical model, AI-alignment claim, or ontological hypothesis in the connected repositories.

Its primary question is narrower:

> Does the surviving corpus document a real, chronological, logically connected, and methodologically evolving epistemic development process rather than a purely retrospective narrative?

The present v0.1 captures the major findings reached so far and establishes a controlled base for subsequent work.

This baseline must distinguish at least four states:

1. **Erkenntnisstand** — what had been understood or recognized.
2. **Explizitationsstand** — what had been made explicit in language or formal structure.
3. **Materialisierungsstand** — what had been persisted as an artifact, repository, registry, specification, code, or governance object.
4. **Audit-/Governance-Stand** — what had been reviewed, classified, frozen, qualified, or formally controlled.

These states must not be collapsed into one another.

A repository status such as OPEN, DRAFT, PRE-AUDIT, or NOT CANONICAL is not evidence that the underlying insight did not yet exist. Conversely, a persisted artifact is not evidence that its scientific content is true.

# 2. Evidence status taxonomy

The EPA uses the following evidence states:

**VERIFIED**  
Directly supported by fixed repository metadata, commit history, artifact content, hashes, or other primary material inspected during the audit.

**SUPPORTED**  
Multiple pieces of primary evidence fit the interpretation, but at least one causal, semantic, or historical step remains inferential.

**INFERRED**  
A reasonable reconstruction from the surviving record, not yet sufficiently bound to primary evidence.

**UNRESOLVED**  
Evidence is incomplete, ambiguous, or not yet examined.

**CONTRADICTED**  
Primary evidence conflicts with the proposed reconstruction.

The audit must also distinguish:

- prospective contemporaneous records;
- contemporaneous but interpretive records;
- retrospective reconstruction;
- current-state documents that may contain later conceptual repairs.

Historical states must be frozen at time t before later knowledge is projected backward.

# 6. Dual dependency model

The EPA must maintain two separate graphs.

## 6.1 Epistemic Genesis Graph

A →E B means:

Work or insight A was historically part of the path by which B became visible or materialized.

Examples:

TIG / TIG-E work →E recognition of general Foundation structure.  
IMMUNIT / TIG-E Foundation work →E Ex-I>0 repository separation.  
HT delta →E SSC state-control projection.

## 6.2 Structural / Authority Dependency Graph

A →S B means:

In the current model, B is intended to depend formally, semantically, or architecturally on A.

Examples:

Foundation →S TIG-E domain projection.  
TFN Foundations →S HT target/architecture.  
Shared controlled source →S dependent current artifact.

A historical discovery path may therefore run opposite to the current structural dependency.

This distinction is mandatory to prevent false contradiction.

# 7. Dependency edge classes

The current EPA uses at least four edge classes.

**N — Necessary / explicit dependency**  
The downstream object explicitly names the upstream object or relation as required input.

**E — Strong enabling dependency**  
The upstream state strongly enables the downstream transition, but alternative historical paths cannot be excluded.

**T — Temporal/contextual association**  
Chronology or shared context is established, but causal interpretation is not yet justified.

**C — Capability dependency**  
The downstream work depends not on scientific content from the earlier artifact but on a reusable capability learned or materialized through earlier work.

Examples of C-type dependencies:

- Riemann does not scientifically depend on TIG-E, but its governance-first initialization is consistent with previously accumulated governance capability.
- IMMUNIT imports methodology and governance while explicitly rejecting automatic claim transfer.
- current multi-context Human–AI orchestration depends on recovery, role, and state-control capabilities rather than on any single physics claim.
