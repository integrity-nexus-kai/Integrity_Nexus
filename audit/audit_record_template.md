# Audit Record Template

Use this template for every audit performed under the TIG Research Governance Standard.

```text
Audit ID:
Date and time:
Repository:
Fixed snapshot / commit:
Auditor or review system:
Assurance level:
Independence:
Relevant conflicts:
Competence scope and limits:
Included paths:
Excluded paths:
Governing standards:
Evidence paths:
Outcome:
Findings:
Unresolved uncertainty:
Correction conditions:
Required handoff:
```

Historical audit entries that lack these fields remain historical records. They must be marked incomplete under the current standard rather than silently upgraded.
