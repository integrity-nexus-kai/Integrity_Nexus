# Competence, Risk, and Collaboration Standard

## Competence Boundary

Each material artifact must identify the competence used, material competence limits, and the qualified review required beyond those limits. Independent research status does not waive subject-matter review requirements.

## State-of-the-Art Review

Research claims must document the relevant literature search scope, date, sources consulted, contrary evidence, credible alternatives, and unresolved limitations. A repository bibliography is not by itself evidence that this review occurred.

## Risk and Safeguard Record

Before implementation or external release, assess the applicability of:

- research ethics;
- privacy and personal data;
- safety and security;
- dual use and misuse;
- export control and legal restrictions;
- societal and environmental impact;
- licensing and intellectual property.

Each item must be `APPLICABLE`, `NOT APPLICABLE` with reason, or `NOT YET ASSESSED`. Material applicable risks require mitigation, explicit acceptance by the responsible human, or a stop condition.

## Collaboration Record

Before material joint work is represented as an accepted collaboration, record goals, participants, roles, decision rights, authorship expectations, data handling, confidentiality, IP, integrity rules, conflict handling, publication approval, and termination conditions.

Informal discussion or external feedback is not automatically a collaboration, endorsement, co-authorship, or independent review.
