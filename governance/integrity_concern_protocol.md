# Integrity Concern Protocol

## Boundary

This protocol covers suspected fabrication, falsification, plagiarism, concealed conflicts, material record manipulation, and serious procedural breaches. Scientific disagreement and honest error are not misconduct by definition.

## Safe Intake

- Do not submit allegations, personal data, confidential evidence, or sensitive correspondence through public GitHub issues.
- Use an author-approved private contact channel identified in `AUTHOR.md` for initial routing.
- Record only a non-sensitive case identifier in public governance material when a public correction becomes necessary.
- Preserve the relevant fixed snapshot before any substantive assessment.

## Required Procedure

1. Register the concern privately without presuming guilt.
2. Preserve evidence and access history.
3. Separate scientific disagreement, honest error, and alleged misconduct.
4. Record conflicts and competence limits.
5. Obtain a qualified reviewer not responsible for the disputed work whenever available.
6. Give the affected person notice and a documented response opportunity.
7. Apply a stated evidence standard and record reasons.
8. Correct the public research record proportionately.
9. Permit reconsideration for material new evidence or procedural error.
10. Protect confidentiality and good-faith reporting to the extent possible.

## Public Record Boundary

Public outcomes belong in `audit/post_publication_actions.md`. Private case material remains outside the public repository under access control. The public register must not expose allegations, health data, private communications, or identifying information beyond what is necessary for a published correction.
